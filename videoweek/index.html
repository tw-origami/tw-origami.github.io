📺 Video of the Week

Hand-picked videos for the week — one click to watch, with no suggested videos and no wandering off to YouTube.

🧑‍🍳 For grown-ups — how to add videos

Open videoweek/data.js and add a line to the videos list — the top of the list is this week's featured pick. Each entry looks like:

{ url: 'paste the YouTube link', title: 'A title', by: 'Channel', note: 'Why it's worth watching', date: '2026-08-19' },

You can paste any YouTube link (youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtu.be/…, or shorts/…) — or just the 11-character video ID. Only url and title are required. Save and refresh.

About ads: suggested videos and links to YouTube are blocked here, but whether a video shows ads is decided by that video's owner and can't be turned off from outside — so pick channels that keep it clean.
